# Personas

## Rules

- Be honest about the evidence. If there's no user research, call them
  **proto-personas** (assumption-based) and list what to validate.
- Never invent statistics or quotes. Paraphrased real feedback is fine if
  its source is named.
- 2–4 personas max. Fewer, sharper personas beat many vague ones.

## Each persona

- Name and one-line summary
- Context: situation, when and how they arrive
- Goals (what they're trying to get done)
- Frustrations / barriers
- What they need from us to act (content, proof, reassurance)
- Key tasks on the site, ranked
- Evidence & confidence (research-based / assumption) and what to validate

Save to `docs/research/personas.md`.
